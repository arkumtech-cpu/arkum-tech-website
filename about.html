// COMPANY PROFILE

A system integrator built around engineering rigor.

Arkum Tech is a security solutions provider specializing in the design, installation, and lifecycle support of integrated security systems. We bring engineering discipline to every deployment — from initial site survey through final commissioning and ongoing maintenance.

Our work spans IP video surveillance, access control, time and attendance, entrance control, and the structured networking infrastructure these systems depend on. Each project is scoped, specified, and executed against international standards.

We position ourselves as a single-source integrator: one accountable partner for specification, supply, installation, and support — eliminating the coordination overhead and gaps that fragment multi-vendor deployments.
